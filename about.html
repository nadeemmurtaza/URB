Our story

A bouquet is a small composition.

URB began as a simple idea: treat each bouquet like a piece of design, with one idea, a limited palette and nothing extra.

We work in small batches and name every design, so a Pink Whimsy or a Blue Hour is something you can ask for again. We also sell through the Pluto marketplace, where the same bouquets are listed.

Delivery is tied to the day you choose, and every order includes a handwritten card.
