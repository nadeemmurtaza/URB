Delivery & care

Delivery fees

Fees are shown on every bouquet: Rs 180 for the smallest, Rs 360 for most, Rs 550 for the largest. With several bouquets in one bag, the highest fee applies once.

Timing

Choose tomorrow or any later date, in a morning (9am to 1pm) or evening (4pm to 8pm) slot. Bouquets are tied the day they leave.

Substitutions

Flowers are seasonal. If a stem is unavailable we swap for the same colour and shape and tell you before it leaves.

Payment

Cash on delivery, bank transfer or card link. You are never charged before we confirm your order.

Caring for your flowers

Trim 2 cm from the stems at an angle, use cool clean water, change it every two days, and keep the vase away from direct sun, heat and ripening fruit.
