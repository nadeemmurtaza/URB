Delivery & care

Every order arrives in the urb box.

Delivery fees

Fees are shown on every bouquet: Rs 180 for the smallest, Rs 360 for most, Rs 550 for the largest. With several bouquets in one bag, the highest fee applies once.

Timing

Choose tomorrow or any later date, in a morning (9am to 1pm) or evening (4pm to 8pm) slot. Each bouquet is wrapped and ribboned before it leaves.

Made by hand

Every stem is crocheted, so small differences in shape or yarn shade are part of the piece. If you want a colour changed, use the custom bouquet form.

Payment

Cash on delivery, bank transfer or card link. You are never charged before we confirm your order.

Caring for your bouquet

Crochet needs no water. Keep it dry, out of direct sun and away from damp. Dust gently with a soft brush or a cool hairdryer, and store it wrapped and flat if you pack it away.
